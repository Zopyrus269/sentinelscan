Responsible Disclosure Policy

Guidelines on authorized assessments, safety, and reporting platform vulnerabilities.

Authorized Assessments Only

SentinelScan strictly performs passive, read-only reconnaissance against targets. We do not exploit vulnerabilities or attempt any form of unauthorized access.

You may only use SentinelScan to assess targets that you own or for which you have explicit, written authorization from the owner. Unauthorized scanning is prohibited and violates our Terms of Service.

Reporting Platform Vulnerabilities

We take the security of the SentinelScan platform seriously. If you believe you have discovered a security issue in the SentinelScan platform itself (not in a scan target), we ask that you report it to us privately to allow us time to resolve the issue before public disclosure.

Please report vulnerabilities directly to our security team:

security@sentinelscan.example — replace with the team's real contact before going live

Safe Harbor

We consider security research conducted in good faith and in accordance with this policy to be authorized. We will not initiate or support legal action against researchers for accidental, good-faith violations of this policy, nor will we treat such reports as a hostile act.
