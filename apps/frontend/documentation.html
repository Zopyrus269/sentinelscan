Getting Started

Open the dashboard to launch scans, monitor progress, and review completed reports for authorized website security assessments.

Authorized Scanning

SentinelScan is intended for use only on systems you own or are explicitly authorized to assess. Always obtain explicit permission before scanning any asset.

How it works

  • Enter an authorized target and confirm ownership.
  • SentinelScan launches the assessment and tracks progress in the dashboard.
  • Download reports and review findings after the scan completes.
